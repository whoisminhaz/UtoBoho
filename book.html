Frequently Asked Questions

Can I extend my booking?

Bookings are strictly for 1 hour to accommodate all guests. If the café is not full, you are welcome to stay longer. Call +60 12-423 7147 to check.

What if I am late?

We hold tables for 15 minutes past the booking time. If you expect to be later, please call us at +60 12-423 7147.

Can I modify my booking?

To change the date, time, or number of persons, please call us directly. You can securely cancel your booking anytime using the "Manage Booking" tab above.
